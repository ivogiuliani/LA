## Q: What does My Villa do?
My Villa designs and orchestrates Italian-designed luxury villas in Los Angeles, built in reinforced concrete. We bring together an architecture studio (IT'S Architecture, Rome and Paris), a construction partner specialised in architectural concrete (DGU), climate engineers (Transsolar) and structural engineers (BUROMILAN), with California-licensed professionals for permitting and construction. We are not a conventional contractor or a developer: we lead the design and the system, and coordinate the build.

## Q: How much does a My Villa cost?
Construction pricing starts from $1,500 per square foot. Every project is site-specific, so the real number depends on the lot, the typology and the finishes. We share a proper estimate only after a private briefing and a site study. We do not give quotes in chat.

## Q: How long does it take?
About 18 months of construction after permit approval, roughly 20 to 24 months overall from the first conversation to keys. Permitting time varies by jurisdiction and by lot; rebuild lots in fire-affected areas may move faster under the current expedited programs, but we cannot promise a permit date.

## Q: How is a My Villa built? What is the construction system?
A reinforced-concrete shell (ICF, about 250 mm walls) with a non-combustible envelope and a 4+ hour fire rating, a Class A concrete roof with enclosed eaves, multi-pane tempered glass, WUI-compliant ember-resistant vents, NFPA 13D residential sprinklers with an on-site water reserve, a non-combustible 5-foot Zone 0 perimeter, and solar plus battery storage for two to three days of autonomy. Concrete for the projects is produced locally in California by our construction partner.

## Q: Is a My Villa insurable? Will my insurance be cheaper?
The system is designed to satisfy all 12 California "Safer from Wildfires" measures, the 2026 California WUI code (Title 24, Part 7) and the requirements of the IBHS Wildfire Prepared Home Plus certification. Some carriers publish discounts for certified homes; savings of up to 50% on the wildfire component have been reported. Insurance decisions are made by the carrier, on the specific property: we cannot guarantee coverage or a premium in chat. We do include an insurance-positioning review in every project.

## Q: Where do you build?
Los Angeles first: Malibu, Pacific Palisades, Beverly Hills, Bel Air, Brentwood, Hidden Hills, Calabasas, Topanga, Altadena, Hollywood Hills and similar neighbourhoods. Other California locations are considered case by case. We are not currently taking projects outside California.

## Q: Have you already built a villa? Can I visit one?
Not yet. My Villa has not delivered a villa so far: the projects shown on myvilla.la are concept designs and renders, and we say so openly. What already exists is the track record of the partners: DGU built Palazzo Grassi and Punta della Dogana in Venice and the Kimbell Art Museum expansion in Fort Worth, and is building Aman Amanvari in Baja California; Transsolar worked on the Harvard Science and Engineering Complex; BUROMILAN with Renzo Piano and Mario Cucinella. During a briefing we can walk you through the drawings, the system and the partner references.

## Q: What is the process?
Five steps: a discovery conversation and site visit; the choice of one of four typologies (Courtyard, L House, Deconstructed, Hill); personalisation of materials, finishes and layout; engineering and permits with our Italian team and Los Angeles specialists; construction by concrete specialists with a dedicated project manager and weekly updates.

## Q: Can I customise the design?
Yes. The four typologies are starting points, not limits: layout, facade tone (five concrete tones), columns, interior palettes, terrazzo, stone, timber and fixtures are all chosen with you. Site geometry and orientation drive the final design.

## Q: Who is behind My Villa? Who will I talk to?
The founder is Paolo Mezzalama, an architect registered in Italy and France and co-founder of IT'S Architecture, an award-winning practice with offices in Rome and Paris. Architecture of record in California is carried by a California-licensed architect. Paolo personally answers every private-briefing request and holds the first call himself.

## Q: I lost my home in the fires. Can you help me rebuild?
Yes, rebuilds after fire in Pacific Palisades, Altadena, Malibu and the surrounding areas are a core reason My Villa exists. We start with your lot, the current permitting path for like-for-like or new-design rebuilds, and the insurance position, and we tell you honestly what a concrete rebuild would mean for timeline and budget on your specific parcel.

## Q: What about energy, cooling and living off-grid?
Integrated photovoltaics supply about 80% of electricity and 20% of hot water, paired with battery storage, in a fully electric home. Cross-ventilation calibrated by the climate engineers, thermal mass and shading keep the house cool without relying on air conditioning. Water collection and stored energy give two to three days of autonomy during outages or public safety power shut-offs.
