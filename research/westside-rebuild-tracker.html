20 months after the Palisades Fire, the rebuild is visible one permit at a time. This page counts them, and shows what the permits themselves say about how the Westside is being rebuilt.

What the permits say

Of the 2,719 new 1–2 family building permits filed in the four areas since 2025-01-08, 1,776 carry the LADBS "2025 wildfire rebuild" work description, almost all of them in Pacific Palisades. 1,640 permits are issued and presumably under construction, 64 have reached a Certificate of Occupancy or final, and 1,012 are still in plan check or verification.

On construction type, the permits are explicit. Where a type is declared, 99.4% (2,479 permits) are Type V, the wood-frame category, and 0.4% (11) are Type I or II, the non-combustible category that covers reinforced concrete and steel structures. We publish this share without editorial spin: it is the clearest public measure of how much of the Westside is being rebuilt with the same structural logic as before.

Method

  • Source. City of Los Angeles Open Data portal, dataset Building and Safety - Building Permits Submitted from 2020 to Present (id gwh9-jnip), queried through the public SODA API without a token. The companion Permits Issued dataset (pi9x-tg5x) was used to verify the fields. LADBS refreshes the data weekly.
  • Filter. permit_type = "Bldg-New", permit_sub_type = "1 or 2 Family Dwelling", submitted_date ≥ 2025-01-08, ZIP codes 90049, 90077, 90210, 90272, 91316, 91356, 91436. Additions, alterations, grading, pools and demolitions are excluded. Accessory dwelling units and garages filed as separate new-building permits are included and shown by use in the CSV.
  • Areas. Pacific Palisades = 90272; Brentwood = 90049; Bel Air / Beverly Crest = 90077 and the City of Los Angeles portion of 90210; Encino / Tarzana = 91316, 91356, 91436. The CSV keeps the LADBS community plan area and hillside flag for each permit.
  • Wildfire rebuild flag. Set when the permit's work description contains "WILDFIRE REBUILD", the wording LADBS uses for post-fire reconstruction.
  • Construction type. Taken from the permit's construction field (California Building Code Types I–V) exactly as declared by the applicant.
  • Status. LADBS status_desc grouped into four buckets: in plan check, issued, completed (CofO issued or permit finaled), withdrawn / inactive.
  • Refresh. Rebuilt automatically every Monday at 05:30 UTC. Retrieval date is printed on the chart and in the JSON.

Limits

Malibu is not here. Malibu is an incorporated city with its own building department; its permits are not in LADBS data. The same applies to Topanga and other unincorporated areas handled by Los Angeles County, and to the City of Beverly Hills proper (only the City of Los Angeles part of ZIP 90210 appears).

A permit is not a house. A submitted permit may be revised, withdrawn or never built; an issued permit marks the start of construction, not its end. Several permits can refer to one lot (main house, ADU, garage).

Construction type is self-declared on the application and can change at plan check. We report it as filed.

The current month is incomplete, and the portal's weekly refresh may lag City records by several days.
