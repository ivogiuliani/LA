02

When a My Villa fits your client.

Budget
$1,500 to $3,500 per square foot, turnkey and furnished, land excluded. For reference, a 5,000 sq ft villa sits between $7.5M and $17.5M before land. The band reflects the level of finish and furniture.
Lot
Malibu, Beverly Hills, Bel Air, Brentwood, Hidden Hills, Calabasas, Pacific Palisades and comparable neighbourhoods. Flat, corner, long or hillside lots each have a typology. Rebuilds after fire are a core case.
Timeline
About 18 months of construction after permit approval, 20 to 24 months overall. Clients who want keys within a year are better served elsewhere, and we will say so.
Priorities
A permanent home rather than a quick resale: architectural concrete, insurability by design, Italian craftsmanship. Clients who want a catalogue house or a spec build are not our clients.

What we need from you to be useful

  • The lot, or the target areaAddress or neighbourhood, current state, whether it is a rebuild.
  • The programApproximate square footage, bedrooms, special rooms: studio, guest house, wellness.
  • The timelineWhen your client would like to start, and any date that matters.
  • The budget bandTurnkey, before land. A range is enough.

With these four points we prepare the briefing properly: which typology fits, what the site suggests, what the range would be.
