A California homeowner's post going around X this week captures the new math: replacement cost has outrun what most policies — written years ago — are structured to cover. The gap between insured value and actual rebuild cost is where families get caught.

It's also why build-once, build-right is becoming a financial argument as much as an architectural one. Reinforced concrete carries a higher upfront cost and a dramatically different long-tail risk profile.
