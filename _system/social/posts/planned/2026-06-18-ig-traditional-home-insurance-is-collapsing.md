The insurance market isn't just hardening in California — it's being rewritten from scratch.

A new piece in Architectural Digest (@archdigest) tracks the quiet rise of AI-assisted parametric insurance: policies that pay out automatically when a measurable event (wind speed, fire perimeter, shake intensity) crosses a threshold — no adjusters, no multi-year claims. It's exploding across disaster-prone regions and now circling FEMA's territory.

The takeaway for LA homeowners: as carriers retreat, the cost of risk is being priced into the house itself. Materials, structure, and resilience aren't lifestyle choices anymore — they're underwriting inputs.
