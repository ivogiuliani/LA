The construction system

Architectural concrete, engineered for fire, insurability and permanence.

What is built, how it performs, who builds it and how it is checked. The technical basis of every My Villa, in one page.

01

Structure.

A reinforced-concrete shell that is the finish, the insulation and the fire barrier at the same time.

Walls
Double-skin precast panels: two fair-faced architectural concrete slabs, pigmented with mineral oxides in five signature tones, an insulation layer between them, and a reinforced-concrete core cast in place on site. The finished surface is the structure: no cladding, no render, nothing to burn or replace.
Floors and roof
Lightened concrete slabs on electro-welded lattice girders. Class A concrete roof with enclosed eaves and non-combustible soffits.
Concrete
DGU's approach: mobile plants and on-site production, which limit transport emissions and chemical additives and let the mix be optimised for the location; recovery and reuse of production waste; a material chosen for durability, to reduce the obsolescence of the building.

Performance

  • 4+ hour fire ratingNon-combustible envelope. The structure itself is the fire barrier.
  • Thermal massConcrete stores the night's cool and releases it by day; with cross-ventilation and shading calibrated by Transsolar, the house stays comfortable without relying on air conditioning.
  • A century of serviceReinforced concrete designed for a service life measured in generations, with maintenance limited to the ordinary.
02

Six layers of fire protection.

From the structure to the landscape, each layer is a requirement of the codes and standards that insurers read.

  1. Concrete shell

    Reinforced-concrete walls with a 4+ hour fire rating. No wood frame, no combustible cladding.

  2. Windows and vents

    Multi-pane tempered glass. WUI-compliant, ember-resistant vents on every opening: the most critical ember-entry measure in the IBHS research.

  3. Roof and eaves

    Class A concrete roof. Enclosed boxed eaves and non-combustible soffits, so embers cannot accumulate at the roof-wall junction.

  4. Sprinklers and water

    NFPA 13D residential sprinklers as standard, with an on-site water reserve independent of the municipal supply.

  5. Defensible space

    Non-combustible five-foot Zone 0 perimeter and fire-smart Mediterranean planting: olive, lavender, succulents.

  6. Autonomy

    Solar and battery storage for two to three days: during public safety power shut-offs and evacuations, pumps, sprinklers and communications keep working.

03

Insurable by design.

Safer from Wildfires
The California Department of Insurance framework of twelve mitigation measures. By regulation, insurers must offer discounts for the measures adopted. A My Villa is designed to satisfy all twelve.
2026 California WUI Code
Title 24, Part 7, mandatory from 1 January 2026 for new construction in Fire Hazard Severity Zones. The concrete system meets the structural requirements natively.
IBHS Wildfire Prepared Home Plus
The most demanding voluntary certification recognised by carriers. The villa is designed to its requirements from the first sketch; certification is issued on the built home.

Some carriers publish discounts for certified homes; savings of up to 50% on the wildfire component of the premium have been reported. Insurance decisions are made by the carrier, on the specific property: we do not guarantee coverage or a premium. We do include an insurance-positioning review in every project, and we work with your broker from the start.

04

Energy and comfort.

Electricity

Integrated photovoltaics supply about 80% of the electricity and 20% of the hot water of a fully electric home, paired with battery storage.

Cooling

Cross-ventilation, thermal mass and shading, calibrated by Transsolar for each site and orientation, keep the house cool without relying on air conditioning.

Autonomy

Water collection and stored energy give two to three days of autonomy during outages and public safety power shut-offs.

06

How quality is checked.

A documented process, to market standards, with licensed professionals at every step that requires one.

  • Mock-ups before productionFull-scale façade panels are produced and approved for colour, texture and joints before the walls are cast.
  • Samples signed offStone, terrazzo, timber, metals and fabrics are approved on physical samples, and the approved set becomes the contract standard.
  • Mix design testedThe concrete mix is tested for pigment, finish and strength on the site's own aggregates before production.
  • Inspections of recordStructural, fire and energy inspections required by the jurisdiction are carried out by the licensed firms of record.
  • Weekly reportsOne project manager, a weekly report with photographs and the updated schedule, and a decision log your advisors can read.
