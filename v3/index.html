01

What we do, and for whom.

My Villa is the Los Angeles practice of IT'S Architecture, Rome and Paris. We design the villa, define its construction system and select the partners who deliver it.

Design, structural and climate engineering come from our Italian and European partners. Permitting, engineering of record and construction in California are carried out by licensed local firms, accredited in Los Angeles, that we select for each project. We work only with licensed professionals: it is the standard of the market, and it is ours.

Every villa is built to the highest level of quality: architectural concrete, natural stone, bespoke joinery and Italian craftsmanship throughout. There is no economy version.

For whom

  • Private clientscommissioning a new villa or a rebuild in Malibu, Beverly Hills, Bel Air, Brentwood, Hidden Hills, Calabasas, Pacific Palisades and comparable neighbourhoods.

02

Four typologies. One for each kind of lot.

Every project starts from one of four proven configurations, then is redrawn for the site, the orientation and the way you live. The plans below are concept studies at the same scale.

№ 1The Courtyard House

Living spaces on the ground floor around an open-sky courtyard, bedrooms above. For flat lots where privacy matters more than the view.

№ 2The L House

A long living wing and a service wing at a right angle, sheltering the garden and the pool from the street. For corner and wide lots.

№ 3The Deconstructed House

A sequence of independent rooms connected by circulation, each with its own structure and light. For long lots, and for clients who want separate volumes: guests, studio, family.

№ 4The Hill House

Three levels stepping with the terrain: garage and services below, living in the middle, bedrooms above, each with its own horizon. For hillside lots in Bel Air, the Palisades and Malibu.

Façade tone, column profile, stone, timber, interior palettes and furniture are chosen with you. Materials and personalisation

05

How we work.

Five steps, one point of contact. Your agent, attorney, business manager or broker is welcome from the first call.

  1. Discovery and site visitMonth 1

    A conversation about the lot, the program and how you live. We visit the land and study orientation, sunlight, wind and context.

  2. TypologyMonth 2

    One of the four configurations, calibrated to the geometry of the lot and to the view.

  3. PersonalisationMonths 2 – 4

    Layout, façade tone, columns, stone, timber, interiors and furniture. Every choice is documented with samples and mock-ups.

  4. Engineering and permitsFrom month 4

    Structural and climate engineering by BUROMILAN and Transsolar. Permits and engineering of record by licensed California firms.

  5. Construction and delivery18 months after permits

    Architectural concrete by specialists, a dedicated project manager, weekly reports. Keys with the furniture in place.

Scope, price and timeline are fixed at contract. Any change is priced before it is made.
