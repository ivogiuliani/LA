Hazard insurance has crossed the line from cost center to market constraint.

A new HousingWire analysis lays out how premium spikes and carrier pullbacks are starting to dictate where transactions clear — and where they don't. For high-value LA neighborhoods exposed to wildfire risk, that pressure increasingly flows back to one question: what is the home actually made of?

Reinforced concrete shifts the underwriting conversation. Non-combustible assemblies, lower replacement-cost volatility, and documented resilience are becoming part of how lenders, insurers, and buyers price a home.
