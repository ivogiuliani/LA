Hardening works — when it's done before the fire.

A widely-shared thread via @goaloa documents Pacific Palisades residents who installed fireproof roofs, exterior sprinklers, and proper home-hardening measures — and saved their homes while neighbors lost theirs.

The lesson isn't luck. It's specification: non-combustible envelopes, ember-resistant vents, defensible space, and active suppression. Reinforced concrete construction extends that logic to the structure itself.
