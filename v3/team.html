02

The studio.

IT'S Architecture is a research- and innovation-oriented practice with offices in Rome and Paris, working from urban planning to design. Principal completed and ongoing projects include the redevelopment of Piazza dei Cinquecento and the urban space of Termini station in Rome; the renovation of the former school of architecture in Nanterre, on the Pôle Léonard de Vinci campus in Paris; the renovation of the former INPS headquarters in Via dell'Amba Aradam, Rome; the new headquarters of the MSA, Mutualité Sociale Agricole, in Bordeaux; experimental housing in EUR, Rome; and the FOROF centre for contemporary art by Giovanna Caruso Fendi in Rome.

Recognition: NAJA, French Ministry of Culture, 2008; Young Architecture Talent, National Council of Italian Architects, 2014; BIM & Digital Award, 2017; Inventons la Métropole du Grand Paris 2, 2019; C40 Reinventing Cities, Rome, 2023.

its.vision

My Villa

My Villa is the studio's Los Angeles practice, with a single brief: luxury villas in architectural concrete that stay standing, insurable and livable through the fire cycles ahead. The Italian villa typology, courtyard, podium, portico, pergola, is reinterpreted for Malibu, Beverly Hills and the Westside.

In California

Permitting, engineering of record and construction are carried out by licensed local firms, accredited in Los Angeles, that we select for each project. We work only with licensed professionals.
