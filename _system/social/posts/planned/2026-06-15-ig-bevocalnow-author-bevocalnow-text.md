72,000 policies cancelled — months before the fires.

A post via @bevocalnow underscores what's become the defining backdrop to California homeownership: State Farm, the state's largest insurer, dropped 72,000 policies in the months leading up to the LA wildfires. The retreat wasn't reactive. It was predictive.

When insurance becomes scarce, construction quality becomes the new underwriting. Materials that meaningfully reduce loss probability — reinforced concrete among them — start to matter in ways they didn't a decade ago.
