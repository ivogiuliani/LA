Last updated: September 2026

It is written to satisfy the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) notice-at-collection requirements, in plain English.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is My Villa, the Los Angeles practice of IT'S Architecture.

For any privacy question or request, write to info@myvilla.la. We answer from that same address.

2. What we collect

Briefing and contact forms. When you request a private briefing we ask for your first name, email address and three quick choices (type of project, timeline, how you found us). Optionally you may add your last name, phone number, the location of your site, who referred you, a short message, and whether you would like occasional insights from us. The form also records technical context that helps us understand where a request comes from: the page you submitted it from, the page you arrived on, the referring site and any campaign parameters in the address (utm_*). The form contains a hidden anti-spam field that real visitors never see or fill.

Email. If you write to us, we keep the correspondence, including your address and anything you choose to share.

AI assistant (when active). If the Site offers a chat assistant, it is clearly labelled as automated. What you type is used to answer you and may be reviewed by our team to improve answers and, if you ask for a briefing, to prepare it. Please do not type sensitive information into it.

Analytics, with your consent where required. With Google Analytics 4 we measure pages viewed, approximate location (city level), device and browser type, the referring site and interactions with buttons and forms (for example that a form was started or submitted, never what you typed into it). IP addresses are not stored by Google Analytics 4. See section 8.

Server logs. Our hosting providers keep standard access logs (IP address, time, requested page, user agent) for security and operations.

We do not collect financial information, government identifiers or special categories of data through the Site, and we ask you not to send them.

4. Who processes your data

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. We use the following service providers, each bound by a data processing agreement or equivalent terms:

  • Formspree (United States) receives form submissions and forwards them to our mailbox.
  • Google Workspace (Google LLC, United States, with EU data-region options) hosts our email, calendar and document storage; Google Analytics 4 provides the site measurement described in section 8.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States) hosts the public Site and keeps access logs.
  • A virtual private server hosted in the European Union runs the private tools with which our team receives, records and answers requests.
  • Anthropic (Anthropic, PBC, United States) provides the language model we use to draft replies and to classify requests. Drafts and classifications are always reviewed by a person before anything is sent or decided. Anthropic does not train its models on data sent through its API.
  • Google Fonts delivers the typefaces of the Site and receives the technical request to do so.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it, or to professional advisers bound by confidentiality.

5. International transfers

My Villa's team works between Los Angeles and Europe, and several providers above are in the United States. Where data leaves the EEA, the UK or Switzerland we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, and, for providers certified under it, on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (including its UK and Swiss extensions). You can ask us for details of the safeguard that applies to a given provider.

6. How long we keep it

  • Briefing requests and related correspondence: 24 months from the last exchange, unless a project starts, in which case the data becomes part of the project file.
  • Insights emails: until you unsubscribe or until we have not heard from you for 24 months.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics 4 retains event data for 14 months; aggregated reports may be kept longer.
  • Server and email logs: as set by the provider, typically 30 to 90 days.

You can ask us to delete your data earlier at any time (section 7).

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive it in a portable format, to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting what was done before), and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. California residents additionally have the right to know, to delete, to correct, to opt out of sale or sharing (we do neither) and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

To exercise any right, email info@myvilla.la from the address you used with us, or tell us how we can verify it is you. We answer within one month (GDPR) or 45 days (CCPA), and we never charge for a first request. You may use an authorised agent under the CCPA; we will ask for proof of the authorisation.

8. Cookies, analytics and consent

The Site uses Google Analytics 4, which sets first-party cookies (_ga, _ga_*) to recognise a browser across visits and to measure use of the Site. We use Google's Consent Mode:

  • If your browser reports a European time zone (EEA, UK, Switzerland), no analytics cookie is set until you click Accept in the banner. If you click Decline, Google Analytics only receives cookieless, aggregate signals that do not identify your browser.
  • Elsewhere, analytics cookies are set by default and a small notice points here, where you can opt out.
  • We never use advertising cookies, and advertising features of Google Analytics are switched off.

Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage (key myvilla_cookie_consent) and in a session value that remembers where you arrived from during a visit; neither is sent to us unless you submit a form. Google Fonts may set its own technical cookies. You can also block cookies entirely in your browser settings; the Site works without them.

9. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of sale or sharing of personal information, which we do not do in any case, and as a decline of analytics cookies for that browser. Browsers' older Do Not Track setting has no agreed meaning; we honour it in the same way as GPC on this Site.

10. Security

The Site is served over HTTPS. Form submissions travel encrypted to Formspree and to our own request endpoint. Access to the mailbox and the tools that hold requests is limited to the My Villa team, protected by two-factor authentication, and the records live outside the public website. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children

The Site is not intended for anyone under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has sent us data, tell us and we will delete it.

12. Changes and contact

We update this notice when our practices or the law change, and we show the date at the top. Material changes to how we use data already collected will be announced on this page before they take effect.
