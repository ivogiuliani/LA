Before the call

Straight answers, before the call.

How long does it take?

Construction takes about 18 months after permit approval, roughly 20–24 months overall. The path is fixed from the first conversation: discovery, typology selection, personalization, engineering and permits, then off-site prefabrication and on-site assembly with a fixed-price agreement.

How do you work with my agent, broker or architect?

As collaborators. Many briefings arrive through a buyer's agent, a broker holding a lot, or a family's architect or advisor. We share feasibility reads, keep your representative in every conversation you want them in, and can work alongside an architect of record you have already chosen. If you are the representative, say so in the form and we will address the briefing to you.

What happens next

Three steps, no pressure.

  1. A reply within one business dayWe read every request and write back with two proposed time windows.
  2. A 30-minute call on Microsoft TeamsA My Villa partner joins to answer every question: your site, your timeline, the system. We listen more than we present.
  3. A feasibility read of your lotFire-hazard zone, insurance positioning, and which villa typology the site could carry — in writing, before any commitment.
